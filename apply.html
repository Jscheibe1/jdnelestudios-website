THE ARTIST DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM

Six weeks. One artist. One finished record.

Weekly one-on-ones with JD across voice, writing and recording, ending with a song you own — recorded and mixed at the studio in Los Angeles. $1,200, with limited spots each month so every artist gets real attention.

Weeks 1–2

Voice and direction. We find what your voice actually does best and pick the record we are going to make.

Weeks 3–4

Writing and pre-production. The song gets written to your story, with the beat locked and the structure tight.

Weeks 5–6

Recording and mix. You cut the record at the studio and walk out with a finished song that is yours.

APPLY
Send your application

Your application goes straight to JD's inbox — copy your answers into the email and hit send. If it is a fit, the free fifteen-minute consult goes on the calendar.

Who this is for

Artists who are serious about leaving with a real record — whether that is your first song ever or the one that finally sounds like you. You do not need experience, equipment or a catalog. You need six weeks and something to say.
