One-on-one coaching

For singers and artists at any level — first-timers included. Sessions cover vocal technique, voice control and breath training, pitch and stamina, songwriting development, and how to actually perform in a booth. JD builds each session around your voice and your goals, not a one-size curriculum.

Every session ends with two things: a recorded clip of where your voice is that day, and one specific thing to drill before the next session. That is how progress gets heard instead of guessed. Virtual sessions run over video from anywhere in the world — a phone and earbuds are all you need.

Good to know

Do I need experience or equipment?

No. Beginners are welcome in every lane. For virtual lessons, a phone and earbuds work fine to start.

How does payment work?

Payment is due at booking. Reschedule up to 24 hours ahead at no charge. Lesson packs are paid up front — that is what the discount is for.

Can I train from outside LA?

Yes — coaching, writing and custom production all run fully virtual. Only studio time needs you in the room.

What about artists under 18?

Minors train with a parent or guardian booking the sessions and present for in-studio work. Plenty of young singers start exactly this way.

Who owns what I make here?

You do. Songs written for you, records you cut, and the program record all belong to you under the terms set before work starts.

How fast do I hear back?

Texts to (310) 293-7598 get the fastest reply. Email and DMs get answered when JD is out of the booth, usually the same day.
